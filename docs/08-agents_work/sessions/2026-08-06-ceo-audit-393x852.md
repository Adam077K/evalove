## Two false alarms I caught on myself

Recorded because this project's most expensive failures are all of this shape.

1. **The dark vertical strip on `/today`'s right edge is the scrollbar thumb**, not a
   layout defect. Thumb length = 852 × (852/1553) = 467px sitting at track bottom, which
   matches the observed y≈385→852 exactly. I was one step from filing it.
2. **"Day and night look identical" was wrong.** I compared two image reads by eye and
   concluded dark mode was unwired. Decoding the actual pixels showed a 23.5% dim. The
   host Chrome follows the OS scheme, so my first "day" capture was already dark —
   `emulateMedia` is mandatory, and eyes are not an instrument.

**A measurement can be as unexamined as a report.** Both of these were caught by
measuring the thing rather than looking at it.

## Method notes for whoever runs this next

- `localhost`, never `127.0.0.1` — different origin, hydration silently never commits.
- Prove hydration before trusting any interaction (`__reactFiber$` on a real node).
- Viewport captures only. Full-page captures paint `position: fixed` at its document
  offset and lie.
- `emulateMedia({colorScheme})` explicitly in both directions; do not trust the default.
- Decode pixels for anything tonal. PIL is available.
