# Window Contraindications

*Which windows are actively **wrong** for which conversation. Source: T5 (evidence-based protocols), 7 rulings.*

> **This is an enforcement rule, not advice.** The site should refuse — or at minimum interrupt —
> when one of these protocols is opened in a contraindicated window. The failure mode it prevents is
> specific and real: starting a vulnerable conversation that a hard stop or exhaustion then truncates
> leaves the more exposed partner with no landing. That is worse than not starting.

## The rulings

### t5-36-questions-full-protocol (36 Questions, full run)

**Never in:** `W1`, `W4`

The escalating-disclosure design depends on finishing what you start — stopping mid-Set-III leaves the more vulnerable partner exposed with no landing. W1 risks starting Set III right as she's falling asleep; W4 guarantees a hard stop mid-protocol.

### t5-eft-hold-me-tight-conversations (Hold Me Tight / EFT)

**Never in:** `W1`, `W4`, `w6 (unless deliberately chosen)`

These conversations surface attachment fears and old wounds. Doing that with a sleepy, about-to-sleep partner (w1) or under a hard 60-minute cap with a return-to-work deadline (w4) risks flooding without time to re-regulate. W6 is usable only if deliberately chosen as the reason he's staying up — not as a default slot, since sleep deprivation impairs the emotional regulation this work requires.

### t5-imago-dialogue-conflict-full (Imago, full conflict version)

**Never in:** `W1`, `W4`

Same logic as above: real content needs both partners regulated and unrushed. Use t5-imago-appreciation-micro instead in these windows.

### t5-gottman-dreams-within-conflict (Dreams Within Conflict)

**Never in:** `W1`, `W4`, `W6`

Gridlock work asks the listener to stay curious and non-defensive about an old sore spot — a hard ask when exhausted. Unlike Hold Me Tight above, w6 is flagged here with no exception: a sleep-deprived nervous system is a worse foundation for this specific exercise than for a simple conversation. If he's staying up for w6, spend it on something lower-conflict.

### t5-gottman-state-of-the-union (State of the Union — concerns portion)

**Never in:** `W1`, `W4`, `W5`

Parts 3-4 ask for real concerns and a request for change — that needs both people resourced. W1 and w4 don't give it time or alertness; w5 is asymmetric enough (one fading, one energized) that the 'both feel equally heard' premise of the meeting breaks down. Reserve for w7.

### t5-eye-gaze-four-minutes (Four-Minute Eye-Gaze Closer)

**Never in:** `W3`, `W4`

Requires holding visual attention on-screen for several minutes, which reads as conspicuously intimate and simply isn't possible mobile (w3) or plausible at a desk/in public (w4).

### t5-gottman-stress-reducing-conversation (Stress-Reducing Conversation)

**Never in:** `W6`

This one otherwise tolerates asymmetry well, but avoid w6 specifically: asking him to stay up specifically to listen to her vent about her day is a bad trade against w6's cost — save w6 for something that couldn't happen at any other time.

## The rule behind the rulings

Every ruling reduces to one of three failure modes:

| Failure mode | Windows | What goes wrong |
|---|---|---|
| **Truncation** | W4 | A hard return-to-work deadline cuts a protocol mid-vulnerability. |
| **Depletion** | W1, W5, W6 | One partner lacks the regulation the exercise requires. W1 she is spent; W5 he is; W6 he is running on no sleep. |
| **Asymmetry breaking the premise** | W5 | Exercises built on 'both feel equally heard' collapse when one is fading and one is energised. |

**W7 (Saturday) is the only window with none of these three properties.** That is why it carries
almost everything demanding — see `coverage-matrix.md`: 20 of the 24 both-partners-alert activities
in the library land on Saturday. Protecting W7 is not a nice-to-have; it is the single point of
failure for the whole deep-talk and intimacy end of the library.

## One ruling worth reading twice

**Dreams Within Conflict is contraindicated in W6 with no exception**, unlike Hold Me Tight which W6
merely gates. T5's reasoning: gridlock work asks the listener to stay curious and non-defensive about
an old sore spot, and a sleep-deprived nervous system is a worse foundation for that specific exercise
than for an ordinary conversation. If he is staying up for W6, spend it on something lower-conflict.

## And one about W6 generally

The Stress-Reducing Conversation is contraindicated in W6 for a different reason — not risk, but
**value**. Asking him to stay up until 3am specifically to listen to her vent about her workday is a
bad trade against what W6 costs. Save W6 for something that could not happen in any other window.
